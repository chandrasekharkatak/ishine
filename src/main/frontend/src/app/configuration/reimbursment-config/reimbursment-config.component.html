Monthly submission window

Employees may submit from the 1st through the configured day for the current month processing cycle. Submissions after that day are still accepted and assigned to the next month cycle. They are queued until the 1st of that cycle month; approvers are notified only after the cycle opens (submission window 1st through the configured day in that month).

Example: 10 = apply allowed on 1st–10th only.
